Privacy Policy

Three.js Lab provides static browser tools for Three.js development. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the site.

When you use file-based tools such as the GLB Viewer, files are read locally in your browser. The current static MVP does not upload your model files to a server.

We may use privacy-conscious analytics or Google Analytics to understand page views, referrers, device types, and general usage patterns. Analytics help us decide which tools to improve.

In the future, the site may display ads through Google AdSense or similar advertising services. Advertising partners may use cookies or identifiers to provide and measure ads.
